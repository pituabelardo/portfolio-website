context — liga u in thirty seconds

the acb's next generation, turned into a league.

liga u is the competition for the under-22 teams of spain's acb clubs, the league behind the liga endesa. real madrid, barça, valencia, unicaja, joventut: they all field a team here. it was born in 2025 from an agreement between spain's sports council (csd), the basketball federation (feb) and the acb. 2026–27 is season two.

it's the stage before the names: the players who'll be in the liga endesa and the euroleague in two or three seasons, while nobody knows them yet.

i lead content and communication for liga u: creative direction, brand strategy, campaigns and community.

01 — the tension

our audience is trying to leave home. the numbers say it can't.

14.5%of young people in spain live on their own. a record low.
98.7%of a young person's salary: what the average rent costs.
30.2years old: the average age of leaving home.

and 76.8% of liga u's tiktok audience is 18–35: the people setting up a first home, or failing to. a league for under-22s is talking to exactly the generation this hits.

source: consejo de la juventud de españa, emancipation observatory, 2026

02 — the insight

the shot didn't go in. someone moved in.

in basketball, a shot that doesn't even touch the rim is a brick. in spanish too: ladrillo. so every brick a liga u player throws becomes one month of rent for someone under 22.

the spanish line, no entró el tiro. entró alguien, turns on one verb: a shot goes in, a person moves in. the worst moment of a game becomes the one the arena cheers.

03 — rules before ideas

one rule, no fine print, checkable on video by anyone.

no rim at allbrick. that's the whole definition.
hits the rim, missesnot a brick. touching iron doesn't count.
blocked shotnot a brick. it stopped being the shooter's.
free throw, no rimbrick. the purest one there is.

the fund is fixed. it's sized above the most bricks a season can produce, so no month depends on someone missing more. no chance, no gambling smell.

it never moves with sales. “liga u pays more rent if you sell more sofas” is the headline that sinks both of us.

we talk in months, never euros. the league runs on public money. a cost figure would become the story.

liga u brick, season 26/27. 24 × 11.5 × 7 cm. compressive strength: high. rim resistance: none.

04 — the brief changed mid-pitch

these brands don't buy visibility. they buy traffic.

v1 sold territory and reputation: pure brand. then the acb's brand lead, who knew these companies, came back with it straight: home retailers live at the bottom of the funnel. anything that doesn't bring people to a store or a site gets skipped by slide three. i kept the idea and rebuilt the product around it.

the sign-up lives on their domain

every person is their session, their data, in their analytics. liga u never sees an email. and the data became a line in the ask: if they keep the whole asset, it's priced.

order online, pick up in store

no minimum purchase, no receipt needed. whoever comes for it walks in on their own two feet, and finds the rest of the store.

28 reasons to come back

a banner buys attention once, at auction. here every matchday creates a new return visit, from september to may.

from the deck: “this document doesn't contain a single reach promise or a single conversion target.”

05 — the system

the circle closes on its own.

  1. 01 court

    a brick is thrown

    no rim. the counter goes up live, in front of the whole arena.

  2. 02 store

    it lands on the shelf

    with a spec tag, next to the real bricks. the person shopping for cement finds it.

  3. 03 web

    adopted on their site

    the tag leads to the brand's domain: adopt a brick, order the plush. their traffic, their data.

  4. 04 home

    someone has a place

    the fund pays real months of rent. the number on your brick is a month already paid.

a miss on the court ends up as the key to a flat.

06 — the pieces

three things to touch, none of them a banner.

adopt a brick

not a raffle. pick a club, leave your email, get pinged every time “your” brick falls. you see your number before you hand over any data: “you'll get month 41. it isn't paid yet.” that's why people hand it over.

tocho, the plush brick

real scale, 22 × 11 × 7 cm. no face, no eyes: deadpan, or it turns into a kids' mascot. sewn label: “do not throw at the basket”. its number is a real month on the counter, so it isn't merch. it's a receipt.

in store

a hanging spec tag (“rim resistance: none”), one line at the foot of every receipt (“3 bricks missed in liga u today. months of rent paid: 37”) and a 22 m² room set, the size of a real first flat.

the rule: it's placed right if the shopper reads it as part of the store first, and only then finds out it's ours.

08 — judgment

the brands we told ourselves not to call.

one testdoes this company charge anyone rent? then it's out. furniture and listings pass. a rental-management firm doesn't.
one deck in the drawera candidate had just been fined €3.6m by spain's consumer ministry, with a tenants' strike called against it. the ministry fining them and the council funding us are the same government. the deck exists because it was asked for; my recommendation was not to call.
we'd vetoed ourselvesearly decks promised “no proptech in this league” as a perk, and then the best housing partner turned out to be a proptech. i surfaced it as one binary decision: exclusivity by product, or by sector.
the unglamorous problemno stats feed counts bricks. so the count happens live, and we show it: the person counting bricks on a tablet is content, not something to hide.

09 — packaging

three ways in. and what we refuse to promise.

full100 monthsall of it in every store, the counter in the arenas, the league's faces and the final six.

season-long, with first refusal for 2027–28. tiers calibrated per brand, from 20 to 200 months. the leroy merlin version shown.

we commit to

sign-ups in their form, total and by city · sessions with a utm per piece and per matchday · outbound clicks from our channels · pick-up codes issued and redeemed · one activation every matchday

we don't promise

footfall, because it can't be measured honestly · sales, roas or cpa · guaranteed reach, with no paid media behind it · a conversion rate we don't control

10 — the edits

what i cut, and the rule it left behind.

“stone” became “brick”everywhere. three object names died with it (they all meant stone). a brick is the basketball word; a stone was half the idea.
cut: the printed pallet14 branded bricks on a pallet means handling sealed stock that's for sale. no store manager signs that.
cut: aisle 22renaming an aisle touches wayfinding: flow, sales, even evacuation.
cut: our own ratiosconversion rates from a previous campaign. show a ratio as proof and the client writes it down as a target.
added to every deckthe whole proposal on one page, and “the five questions you're going to ask us”: the committee's objections, written by us, at least one of them genuinely uncomfortable.

the rule: an idea that makes the client break their own operating rules never gets approved. in store, only things a home store already does every week.
