context — liga u in thirty seconds

the acb's next generation, turned into a league.

liga u is the competition for the under-22 teams of spain's acb clubs, the league behind the liga endesa. real madrid, barça, valencia, unicaja, joventut: they all field a team here. it was born in 2025 from an agreement between spain's sports council (csd), the basketball federation (feb) and the acb. 2026–27 is season two.

it's the stage before the names: the players who'll be in the liga endesa and the euroleague in two or three seasons, while nobody knows them yet.

i lead content and communication for liga u: creative direction, brand strategy, campaigns and community.

01 — the signal

july 2026: the lollipop became a microphone.

chupa chups and vml brasil turned the most recognisable shape in the candy aisle into a working usb mic, with a record button and a stick. it isn't for sale: it's seeded to creators so it turns up in their videos.

the brand stops appearing in the content and becomes the tool the content is made with. a great product idea.

the insight: the mic already existed. it didn't have a steady job.

creator seeding buys moments. a league is seven months, every weekend, live on tv. the mic could clock in.

sources: marketing directo, reason why (jul–aug 2026)

02 — the idea

everything that sounds in liga u sounds through a chupa chups.

the booth

our commentators call all 28 matchdays holding it, on camera.

the flash interview

courtside, sweating, crowd behind. cut vertical within minutes.

the locker room

the coach's talk, the shout after the buzzer. where tv doesn't go.

cámara u

a different player vlogs their week: training, class, home.

el narradol

our comedy creator, who re-voices the week's best plays at full volume.

the street

media day and absurd questions. mic to the face, our best vertical format.

03 — the rule that turns it into a code

there is no other microphone in liga u.

not a spare. not in the podcast. not at the press conference. one rule, held for seven months, is what separates a brand that sponsors from a brand that was already there.

why it matters to the brand: repetition is what turns an object into a symbol. a logo gets seen; a tool gets remembered.

what it protects: the integration lives inside the production. the broadcast graphics were never on sale. that's a house rule, not a negotiation.

04 — activation 01

the first time.

in liga u, kids give the first interview of their lives. nobody knows them yet. in two or three seasons some of them will be in the liga endesa.

the mechanic: that first microphone is always a chupa chups. the clip is archived under the player's name, and released again the day that kid becomes somebody.

why it works

no other brand can say “we were there before anyone” and have the footage to prove it.

why it renews

a fixed-format series and an archive that grows every season. content that gains value with time instead of expiring.

05 — activations 02 and 03

then it gets big. then it goes up into the stands.

the final six, 13–16 may 2027

four days, six teams, one title. the mic does the trophy, the final's mvp and the first question to the champion under the confetti. the photo of the year.

outside: a four-metre lollipop mic at the arena door. a walk-in sculpture, the photo everyone queues for. press, reels and thousands of pictures nobody pays for.

2026 edition: 3.58m digital views, 258,787 tv viewers

the stands, in 14 arenas

one chupa chups with every ticket. and every game, the mic goes up into the crowd: one fan calls one play, live on the broadcast, then cut vertical for social. the stands stop watching and start narrating.

a candy handed out at the door gets eaten and forgotten. a candy that's your ticket onto the broadcast gets told, by the person who lived it.

06 — fit

why chupa chups, and not any other brand.

the audience

you win people over at 12. we have them at 20, phone in hand and out of reach of most ads.

the object

a ball on a stick reads from 20 metres: from the stands, from camera 3, from a six-second vertical crop. few brands own a silhouette that survives that.

the colour

chupa chups red and liga u red are practically the same. the brand doesn't sit on top of the league. it blends in.

and it travels: 37 nationalities on the rosters, and the league was broadcast in 8 countries last season, brazil included — where the lollipop mic was born.

07 — packaging

a creative proposal, not a rate card.

what each side brings

chupa chups: a season fee, lollipop mics for seven months, product for 14 arenas, a brand licence and the final six build.

liga u: 28 broadcast matchdays with national tv, 14 clubs and ~250 players, in-house production, an 18–35 social audience and a monthly report.

no price in the deck, on purpose: it's a first contact. the number goes on the table in the first meeting.

measured every month

brand minutes on screen, per matchday · pieces published with the mic in frame · reach by platform, with demographics · tv audience, game by game · earned press · unprompted mentions: the one that matters.

the second life

vml made the object. we give it the footage: seven months of real live material to build the case film for festivals and trade press.

our precedent: capullos, the final six 2026 campaign. 427,094 instagram views in five days, 14 times the account's follower base, €0 in media.

08 — how i worked

what i cut, and what i added.

cut: the golden mica weekly mvp award. liga u has no mvp of the week, and an idea that needs a made-up award is a made-up idea.
cut: flavour of the weekit put the brand on the broadcast graphics. those aren't for sale.
cut: a pilot matchdaya one-week trial can't become a code. the rule needs the whole season.
rewritten: the seeding slidev1 argued against creator seeding. you don't win a brand by telling it its strategy is wrong. v2 says it in positive: the mic exists, it needs a steady job.
added after feedbackthe international angle (nationalities, broadcast countries, brazil highlighted) and a third activation for the people in the arena.
flagged, not hiddenattendance. the deck promises no crowd numbers we can't evidence, and the answer has to be ready before the meeting.
